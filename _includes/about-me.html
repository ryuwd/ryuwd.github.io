I’m a Software Engineer at CERN in the EP-LBC group, contributing to the design and development of the distributed data processing infrastructure and software stack supporting the LHCb experiment and its physicists.

Until September 2024, I was a PhD student at the University of Edinburgh Particle Physics Experiment Group, working on the LHCb Experiment based at the Large Hadron Collider, CERN.

Until September 2020, I was a Postgraduate MSc Research student at the University of Manchester, where I worked on Detector Alignment for the Mu2e Experiment at Fermilab.

A lot of my research involved the analysis of large quantities of data e.g. acquired by the LHCb detector. I enjoy programming and regularly contribute to software, where I can. My GitHub and my CERN GitLab profiles display some of my recent contributions.
